# Wedding Invitation Scroll Prompts

Generate the clips in this exact order. Together they will feel like one continuous journey through a single floating wedding invitation world.
The first clip may be customized with each client’s logo. Every later clip remains reusable because it contains no names, dates, logos, or text.

## Continuity rules for every clip

- Format: 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920
- Frame rate: preferably 30 fps
- No generated audio; we’ll add one continuous music track later
- One uninterrupted forward-moving camera
- No cuts, fades to black, sideways jumps, or sudden zooms
- Same two white Indian paradise flycatcher birds with long tails
- Same ivory clouds, champagne-gold light, blush-pink and deep-magenta flowers, sage-green leaves, and delicate gold filigree
- Same warm sunrise lighting throughout
- Keep the central 60% relatively calm for HTML text
- Hold every final composition almost still for one second
- Do not generate names, dates, signs, captions, watermarks, or random letters
- Export the last frame of each clip and use it as the starting image for the next clip
